# Current design notes

The hero leads with Work Less, Achieve More and a personal portrait. A clipped circular lower portrait and aligned head layer create depth without text overlays.

Services explain the work, followed by a five-category sample collage. Each slide uses Challenge / What I did / Result shown. Screenshots open in a closable dialog; category arrows sit at the sides. Tool logos form a varied-size puzzle.

The experience strip reads 1+ years Customer service, 5+ years Tech Support, 6+ years Total working experience. All main action labels use Send an email. Contact drafts open in Gmail in a separate tab and require the visitor to press Send.

Testimonials, pricing and explore-sample links were removed. Commitments and FAQ are hidden but retained for later restoration. Their unsupported policies are not presented as active promises. Detailed sample pages remain available at their existing URLs.

Original design uses olive, sage, beige, Georgia and Arial. Motion is subtle and disabled for reduced-motion preferences. The source cleanup removes only unreferenced files; required case-study styles and all visible assets remain.
